# Next Steps

1. **Retention Campaigns**
   - Deploy **win-back offers** (discount or exclusive content) to customers in the top churn-risk deciles.
   - Introduce **loyalty incentives** for Champions and Loyal segments.

2. **Experimentation**
   - A/B test the depth and timing of win-back offers to measure lift.
   - Track incremental revenue from retention campaigns.

3. **Data Enhancements**
   - Integrate marketing touchpoints (email opens, ad impressions) for multi-channel churn drivers.
   - Incorporate product-level engagement signals to refine CLV estimates.

4. **Model Development**
   - Evaluate tree-based or gradient-boosted models for possible accuracy gains.
   - Explore BG/NBD + Gamma-Gamma models for probabilistic lifetime value.

5. **Operationalisation**
   - Automate monthly scoring and push churn-risk segments directly into CRM/ESP.
   - Monitor model drift and re-train every 3–6 months.

---

> **Goal:** Convert analytical insight into an ongoing, automated retention strategy that directly improves lifetime value.
